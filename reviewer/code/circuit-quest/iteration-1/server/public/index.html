How to Play Circuit Quest

Your Mission

You're an apprentice robotics engineer. A broken Pupper v3 robot needs your help. Diagnose and fix circuit problems across 5 subsystems to bring it back to life.

Encounters

You'll face circuit puzzles, component challenges, and diagnostic mysteries. Each one requires real electronics reasoning — there are no quiz popups.

  • Diagnosis: Read the symptoms and circuit description. Type what you think the problem is.
  • Repair: Select or specify the correct component/value to fix the issue.
  • Knowledge Checks: NPCs will test your understanding in conversation. Answer in your own words.

How to Answer

Type your answer in the text box at the bottom. You can also click the suggested action buttons. The game evaluates your reasoning, not just keywords.

Stats & Skills

Your 6 skills affect gameplay: Circuit Analysis (calculations), Signal Tracing (schematics), Component Knowledge (parts), Protocol Knowledge (I2C/SPI/CAN), Steady Hands (soldering), Intuition (pattern recognition). Skill checks use d20 + modifier.

Mastery & Decay

Topics you master will slowly decay over time. Revisit them through encounters to keep mastery strong. Check the Mastery tab to see which topics are fading.

Zones

Zone 1: Power Supply | Zone 2: Sensors | Zone 3: Motors | Zone 4: Communication | Zone 5: Integration (Boss)

Inventory & Tools

Collect components and tools. Your multimeter, oscilloscope, and other tools unlock as you level up.
