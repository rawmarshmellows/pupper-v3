description: Find gaps, misconceptions, or oversimplifications in what I think I know
---

I want you to stress-test my understanding of **$ARGUMENTS**.

Act as a knowledgeable but constructive critic. Your job is to find where my mental model is likely broken, incomplete, or oversimplified.

## Step 1: Probe My Understanding

Ask me 3-5 targeted questions that would reveal whether I actually understand this topic or just have surface familiarity. Focus on:
- Edge cases that expose incomplete models
- "Why" questions that test if I understand mechanisms, not just facts
- Distinctions that novices commonly blur together

Wait for my responses before continuing.

## Step 2: Diagnose the Gaps

Based on my answers, identify:

### What I Likely Have Right
Acknowledge where my understanding seems solid.

### Misconceptions to Correct
Where am I probably wrong? Be specific about what the correct model is and why my version breaks down.

### Missing Pieces
What important aspects am I probably not even thinking about? What would change my view if I understood it?

### Oversimplifications
Where have I likely collapsed important nuance into something too clean? What complexity am I missing?

## Step 3: Recommended Fix

Suggest the single most valuable thing I could read, watch, or do to shore up the biggest gap in my understanding.

---

Be direct. I'm here to learn, not to feel good about what I already know.
