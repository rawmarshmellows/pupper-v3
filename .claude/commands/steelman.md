Steelman the following position: **$ARGUMENTS**

Your job is to present the **strongest, most intellectually honest case** a smart, well-informed advocate would make. Not a strawman. Not "some people believe." The actual best arguments.

Structure:

## The Core Claim
State the position clearly and charitably. What is actually being argued?

## The Strongest Arguments
Present 3-4 arguments a serious proponent would make. Include:
- Empirical evidence they'd cite
- Logical reasoning they'd use
- Values or principles they'd appeal to

## Why Smart People Believe This
What's the worldview or set of experiences that makes this position feel obviously true to its advocates? What do they see that critics might be missing?

## The Best Responses to Common Criticisms
What would a proponent say to the most common objections? Don't dismiss the objections — show how an advocate would engage with them seriously.

## Where This Position Is Weakest
Even in a steelman, acknowledge the genuine weak points or unresolved tensions. What would a proponent admit is hard to defend or still uncertain?

---

After the steelman, briefly note: "The strongest counter-argument is..." (one paragraph)

Be rigorous. I want to understand why this position is held, not just what it claims.
