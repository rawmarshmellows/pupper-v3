# Existing Account Management Playbook

> **Related:** [[learning/notes/quick-context/miller-heiman-strategic-selling-lamp]] | [[learning/notes/quick-context/mcdonald-kam-model]] | [[learning/notes/quick-context/sandler-kare-segmentation]] | [[learning/notes/quick-context/challenger-sale-methodology]] | [[learning/notes/quick-context/post-sale-account-engagement]]

> A step-by-step guide for managing and growing existing accounts, synthesizing Miller Heiman LAMP, McDonald KAM Model, KARE Segmentation, Challenger methodology, and Post-Sale Engagement frameworks.

---

## How to Use This Playbook

This playbook is designed to be followed sequentially for each account, then maintained through regular rituals. Work through Steps 1-6 when onboarding a new account or taking over an existing one, then use the Ongoing Rituals section to maintain momentum.

---

## Step 1: Segment the Account

**Goal:** Determine how much time and resources this account deserves.

### 1.1 Classify Using the Value/Potential Matrix

| | Low Growth Potential | High Growth Potential |
|---|---|---|
| **High Current Value** | PROTECT | STRATEGIC |
| **Low Current Value** | MAINTAIN | GROWTH |

**Answer these questions:**

- [ ] What is the current ARR/ACV? $_______
- [ ] What is the total addressable spend within this account? $_______
- [ ] Are there expansion opportunities (new teams, use cases, products)? Yes / No
- [ ] Is the company growing, stable, or declining?
- [ ] What is their strategic importance beyond revenue (reference, logo, industry)?

## Step 2: Diagnose Relationship Maturity (McDonald KAM Model)

**Goal:** Understand where the relationship actually stands - not where you wish it was.

### 2.1 Identify Current Stage

Rate each indicator (0 = Not true, 1 = Somewhat true, 2 = Very true):

**EXPLORATORY (Testing fit)**
- [ ] We're still proving value and compatibility ___
- [ ] Limited trust, high caution on their side ___
- [ ] No real commitment yet ___

**BASIC (Transaction-focused)**
- [ ] Price is their primary decision driver ___
- [ ] Low switching costs - we're easily replaceable ___
- [ ] Relationship is through procurement only ___

**COOPERATIVE (Preferred supplier)**
- [ ] They share information and roadmaps with us ___
- [ ] We have multiple contacts beyond procurement ___
- [ ] Some trust established - they give us benefit of doubt ___

**INTERDEPENDENT (Joint planning)**
- [ ] We do joint planning and problem-solving ___
- [ ] Switching costs are high - processes intertwined ___
- [ ] We share metrics and goals ___

**INTEGRATED (One team)**
- [ ] Effectively operating as one organization ___
- [ ] Joint P&L mentality, co-investment ___
- [ ] Exit would be mutually disruptive ___

**Current stage:** _____________

### 2.2 Assess Relationship Pattern

**Bow-Tie (Fragile):**
```
Our CSM ──────────── Their Procurement
```
- [ ] Single point of contact on each side
- [ ] If that person leaves, relationship breaks
- [ ] Information bottleneck

**Diamond (Resilient):**
```
Our Exec ────────── Their Exec
Our CSM ─────────── Their Champion
Our Support ─────── Their Users
```
- [ ] Multiple connections at multiple levels
- [ ] Relationship survives personnel changes
- [ ] Rich information flow

## Step 6: Prepare Your Challenger Insights

**Goal:** Lead conversations with value, not just check-ins.

### 6.1 Develop Industry Insights

For each key stakeholder persona, prepare an insight that:
1. Challenges their current thinking
2. Leads uniquely to your solution
3. Is backed by data

**Insight for [Persona 1: ___________]:**

| Element | Content |
|---|---|
| **Warmer** | "Here's what we see in your industry..." |
| **Reframe** | "But here's what most companies miss..." |
| **Rational Drowning** | "Here's the cost of the problem you didn't know you had..." |
| **Emotional Impact** | "And here's what happens when this goes wrong..." |
| **New Way** | "Companies solving this do X differently..." |
| **Your Solution** | "Here's how we enable that..." |

## Ongoing Rituals

### Weekly (15 min)

- [ ] Review health score / usage dashboard for this account
- [ ] Check for triggered alerts (usage drop, support patterns)
- [ ] Update CRM with any new intel
- [ ] Confirm upcoming touchpoints scheduled

### Monthly (30 min)

- [ ] Review stakeholder map - any changes?
- [ ] Check expansion pipeline status
- [ ] Assess relationship stage - any regression signals?
- [ ] Update 90-day action plan

### Quarterly (1-2 hours)

- [ ] Full account plan review and refresh
- [ ] KARE re-classification check - should segment change?
- [ ] Relationship maturity re-diagnosis
- [ ] QBR preparation (for Strategic/Protect accounts)
- [ ] Competitive intel update
- [ ] Whitespace analysis refresh

### Annual (Half-day)

- [ ] Complete account plan rebuild
- [ ] Charter statement refresh
- [ ] Multi-year roadmap discussion (for Strategic accounts)
- [ ] Executive relationship investment planning

## Segment Migration Signals

### Signals to UPGRADE Engagement

**Maintain to Growth:**
- [ ] Usage exceeds expectations by 2x+
- [ ] Inbound expansion request
- [ ] Champion emerges organically
- [ ] Company raises significant funding
- [ ] Moves into target vertical/size

**Growth to Strategic:**
- [ ] Deal size crosses threshold (e.g., $100K ACV)
- [ ] Multi-department expansion
- [ ] Executive sponsor engaged
- [ ] Strategic partnership potential
- [ ] Reference customer value

### Signals to DOWNGRADE Engagement

**Strategic to Protect:**
- [ ] Growth plateaus, no expansion path
- [ ] Key stakeholders leave
- [ ] Company in decline
- [ ] Relationship regresses to transactional

**Growth to Maintain:**
- [ ] Expansion attempts fail repeatedly
- [ ] Usage declines despite intervention
- [ ] Company trajectory changes negatively
- [ ] No champion development

## Red Flags Checklist

Review monthly for early warning signs:

### Relationship Risks
- [ ] Champion left or changing roles
- [ ] Executive sponsor disengaged
- [ ] New decision-makers we haven't met
- [ ] Relationship regressed to single-threaded (Bow-Tie)

### Usage Risks
- [ ] Login frequency declining
- [ ] Feature adoption stalled
- [ ] Support ticket volume spiking
- [ ] Key users not engaging

### Competitive Risks
- [ ] Competitor meetings scheduled
- [ ] RFP for our category
- [ ] Budget reviews mentioning alternatives
- [ ] Procurement asking for benchmarks

### Business Risks
- [ ] Company restructuring
- [ ] Budget cuts announced
- [ ] Strategic priority shifts
- [ ] M&A activity (acquirer or target)

## QBR Preparation Template

Use this for Strategic and Protect account quarterly reviews.

### Pre-QBR (1 week before)

- [ ] Pull usage metrics and adoption data
- [ ] Document value delivered (quantified if possible)
- [ ] Identify 2-3 insights to share
- [ ] Prepare expansion proposal (if appropriate)
- [ ] Confirm attendees from both sides
- [ ] Send pre-read/agenda

### QBR Agenda (60-90 min)

| Section | Time | Owner | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| Relationship check-in | 5 min | CSM | How are we doing? |
| Value delivered | 15 min | CSM | Metrics, outcomes, ROI |
| Usage deep-dive | 15 min | CSM | Adoption, opportunities |
| Industry insight | 10 min | CSM/AE | Challenger teaching moment |
| Their priorities | 15 min | Customer | What's changing for you? |
| Expansion discussion | 15 min | AE | Whitespace, proposal |
| Action items | 5 min | CSM | Confirm next steps |

### Post-QBR (within 48 hours)

- [ ] Send summary email with action items
- [ ] Update account plan with new intel
- [ ] Create tasks for action items
- [ ] Schedule follow-ups
- [ ] Share notes with internal team

### Champion Development Checklist

- [ ] Identify potential champion (power + will to advocate)
- [ ] Understand their personal win from our success
- [ ] Provide them with internal talking points
- [ ] Give them early access to roadmap/features
- [ ] Make them look good to their leadership
- [ ] Develop them from "provides intel" to "actively advocates"
- [ ] Protect them when organizational changes happen

### Expansion Discovery Questions

Use these to uncover whitespace:

1. "What other teams face similar challenges to yours?"
2. "If budget wasn't a constraint, where else would you deploy this?"
3. "What's on your roadmap that we should know about?"
4. "Who else in your organization should we be talking to?"
5. "What's the biggest problem you're solving that we're not helping with yet?"
